IOGuard keeps your preferred Mac audio input, output, and system sounds selected when displays, AirPlay, or reconnects change them.

- Native menu-bar controls and device preferences.
- Pause/resume and optional launch at login.
- One-second delayed restoration, based on a verified Firefox/AirPlay workflow.
- Universal Apple Silicon and Intel app for macOS 13 or later.
- Consistent lock-over-speakers app and DMG artwork.

Install by opening the DMG and dragging IOGuard to Applications. If upgrading from the original script, stop its LaunchAgent before enabling this app (see docs/MIGRATION.md).

Release state: Developer ID signing and notarization results are recorded in docs/VALIDATION.md. This release remains a draft until Apple notarization is complete.
