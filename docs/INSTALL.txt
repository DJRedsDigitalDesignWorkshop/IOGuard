IOGuard — by djred

1. Drag IOGuard into Applications.
2. Open IOGuard from Applications. Look for the lock-and-speakers menu-bar icon.
3. Choose your preferred input and output devices in Settings.
4. Enable the guard. Optionally enable Launch at Login.

The guard restores macOS defaults after about one second. A brief audio switch is possible. If your interface is unplugged, it waits for the device to return. Pause the guard before intentionally selecting another device.

Upgrading from the original Universal Audio Guard script:
Stop the old LaunchAgent before enabling the app. Use the original Uninstall.command in your source folder, or follow docs/MIGRATION.md. Do not run both guards together.

To uninstall: turn off Launch at Login, quit IOGuard, and move it to Trash. No audio drivers are installed or modified.
